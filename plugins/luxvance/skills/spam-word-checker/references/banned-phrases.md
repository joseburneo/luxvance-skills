## Luxvance-specific additions

Brand-guidelines bans (from `.claude/skills/brand-guidelines/SKILL.md`):

```
synergies, synergy, leverage, empower, unlock, revolutionary,
game-changer, ecosystem, world-class, cutting-edge
```

Em-dashes (`—`) are also banned — Luxvance uses commas, periods, or colons.

Flattery openings are banned:

```
I've been following your work, big fan of, longtime admirer,
huge respect for your, I love what you're doing
```
